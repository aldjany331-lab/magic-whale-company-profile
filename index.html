02 — About Magic Whale

We create extraordinary live experiences.

Magic Whale is a creative production company specializing in live entertainment, concerts, music festivals, immersive visual experiences, and large-scale artistic productions.

Approach

We combine artistic direction, production expertise, and advanced visual technologies, turning stages and venues into environments where music, light, visuals, and performance come together.

Delivery

From the initial concept to design, production, and on-site execution, we deliver carefully crafted experiences that captivate audiences and leave a lasting impression.
